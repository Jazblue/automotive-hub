About Jazblue Automotive

One destination.
Four independent projects.

Jazblue Automotive brings together a collection of independent automotive projects created around different parts of the car world. The projects remain separate, while this hub provides one central place to discover them.

From million-pound performance machines to affordable UK cars and practical diagnostics, the aim is simple: make interesting automotive information easier to explore.

UK-focused automotive content Cars, specifications and information relevant to British drivers.
Performance & specifications Explore the numbers behind high-performance and exotic cars.
